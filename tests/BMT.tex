\section{Detection and Navigation Test}
\label{sec:Basic Manipulation Test}

The Detection and Navigation Test (DNT) is executed together with the \iaterm{Basic Manipulation Test}{BMT} of the full
\RCAW. The main focus is to demonstrate basic object recognition and navigation capabilities of robots.

Therefore only two service areas will be used. Those service areas will be located near to each other, e.g. WS3 and WS4
in fig. \ref{fig:arena_example} and \ref{fig:arena_map_annotated}. One service area is used to contain the objects to be
detected, meaning that all objects are placed on this service area. The second service area needs to be reached by the
robot in addition to the Goal.

This test involves no decoy objects.
